# 📊 CloudNova Sales Dashboard – Project Story

## 🏢 The Company
CloudNova Retail Ltd. is a fast-growing lifestyle retailer specializing in:
- Tech gadgets (headphones, smart home devices, tablets)
- Wearables (fitness trackers, smartwatches, smart glasses)
- Clothing (minimal, functional fashion)

They operate both online and through showrooms across four regions:
- North
- South
- East
- West

---

## ❓ The Problem
CloudNova has grown rapidly over the past 10 years. They collect a lot of sales data, but they lack clear answers to questions like:
- Which products or regions are driving profit?
- Is online performing better than retail?
- Are there seasonal sales trends?
- Where should they focus marketing and investment?

---

## 🎯 Project Goal
Build an interactive dashboard that allows CloudNova to:
- Visualize sales, profit, and performance over time
- Filter by year, region, product category, or channel
- Identify trends, gaps, and opportunities
- Make smarter, data-backed business decisions

---

## 🧠 Questions We’ll Help Answer
1. Which product category is most profitable?
2. Which region underperforms?
3. Is online outperforming retail?
4. Are sales improving year over year?
5. What months are the strongest/weakest?

---

## 🧰 Tools Used
- Python
- Pandas
- Streamlit
- Matplotlib/Seaborn
- Git + GitHub

---

## 👨‍💻 Outcome
A professional, interactive sales dashboard built using Python — ready to be shared, demoed, and extended by business or engineering teams.
